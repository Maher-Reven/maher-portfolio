## What it is

A HUD-style incident bridge with three agent panels — scale, revert, blend — all plausible, none obviously wrong. The interactive is pure fiction: no models run, no webhooks fire, every outcome is scripted client-side.

<SyntheticWarRoom />

<LeadershipNote>
When three automated responders disagree, the job is not to pick the smartest patch — it is to decide who owns the risk surface until humans can see clearly. This lab makes that choice legible in about thirty seconds.
</LeadershipNote>

## Why simulate the room

Real war rooms compress judgment under noise: partial telemetry, confident bots, and a clock. EM work shows up in how you **frame** the decision — merge partial fixes, escalate early, or rollback to a known-good state — not in pretending you had perfect information at minute zero. The [decision log](/log) on this site is the archival version; this page is the rehearsal.
