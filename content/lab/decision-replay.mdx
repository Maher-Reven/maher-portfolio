## What it is

A four-step walkthrough, not a quiz. You start in the same room as the case study — mid transition, AI assistants already in use, no shared picture of what an agent may call or ship — then pick one of the three options from the original `<Tradeoff>` block and see a compressed "six months later" consequence.

<DecisionReplay />

Wrong paths are intentional: you can step back and try another fork. The path that matches what shipped surfaces the documented cost before the leadership close.
