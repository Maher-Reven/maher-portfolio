## What it is

Four octaves of simplex noise, warped twice (`fbm(p + fbm(p + fbm(p)))`) to get the liquid look. The cursor injects a Gaussian "push" into the warp offsets, scaled by pointer velocity, so fast movement creates visible turbulence.

## Performance notes

Single full-screen quad, DPR capped at 1.5, no post-processing. Renders comfortably at 60fps on integrated GPUs; reduced-motion users get a static radial gradient instead.
