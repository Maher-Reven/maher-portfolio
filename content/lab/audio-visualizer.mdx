## What it is

An `AnalyserNode` reads the microphone's frequency spectrum roughly 60 times a second; the 128 FFT bins get averaged down to 48 bars and drawn straight onto a 2D canvas, each one damped toward its target so a single loud transient doesn't make the display jitter.

<AudioVisualizer />

## Why the mic, and why it asks first

Nothing here plays a bundled track — it listens to whatever's actually in the room, which is more interesting and skips a hosting/rights question this site doesn't need. It also never requests microphone access on page load. A portfolio silently asking for your mic the moment you scroll past it is exactly the kind of thing this section is supposed to avoid being.
