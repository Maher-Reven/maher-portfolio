## What it is

A `requestAnimationFrame` loop that reveals characters proportionally to elapsed time, filling the unresolved tail with glyphs from a block-drawing set. Spaces are preserved so words keep their rhythm during the scramble.

<LeadershipNote>
  Signature motion is a team identity choice until it ships on every heading — then it's an accessibility surface. Leading motion design means pairing the delight with reduced-motion and performance guardrails before marketing asks to "use it everywhere."
</LeadershipNote>
