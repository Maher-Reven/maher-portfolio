## What it is

A fixed "MK" letterform skeleton — the same one behind the site's handle — jittered by a seeded PRNG into a fresh variation on every load. Same seed, same mark, every time: it's generative, not random. Reproducible, just never predictable in advance.

<GenerativeMark />

## Why straight lines, not curves

Every generative-monogram sketch I tried with smooth curves read as decorative rather than as this site's mark. Faceted, straight-line jitter reads as a signal being redrawn, not a logo being warped — closer to the HUD language the rest of the site already speaks.
