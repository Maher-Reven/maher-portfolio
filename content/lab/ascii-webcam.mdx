## What it is

The camera frame is drawn, mirrored, straight onto a 100-column canvas — the downscale itself does the averaging. Each cell's luminance buckets into `" ░▒▓█"`, the same block-shading glyphs already in the text-decode effect's character set, and the whole grid is written to a single `<pre>` as one string, not drawn glyph-by-glyph.

<AsciiWebcam />

## Why a `<pre>`, not a canvas full of text

A hundred columns is thousands of characters. Calling Canvas2D's `fillText` once per glyph, every frame, is real cost for no benefit — setting one `<pre>`'s `textContent` lets the browser's own text layout do the work instead, the way a terminal actually renders.
