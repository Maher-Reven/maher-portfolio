## The problem

Start with the user or business problem in two or three sentences. What was broken, slow, or missing? Why did it matter?

## The approach

How the team framed it, what you decided *not* to do, and the one or two decisions that shaped the outcome. This is where an engineering manager's judgment shows.

## The result

Concrete outcomes. Numbers if you have them, qualitative signals if you don't.

```txt
before → after
p95 load  2.8s → 0.9s
adoption  12%  → 41%
```

## What I'd do differently

One honest paragraph. Readers trust this section more than any other.
